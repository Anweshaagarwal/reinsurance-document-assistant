# Synthetic treaty review checklist

This synthetic document is for software demonstration only. Treaty review compares the parties, period, territory, covered business, limits, retention, exclusions, reinstatements, reporting duties, premium terms, claims-control clauses, and governing law.

Reviewers compare the signed wording with the placement summary and recorded approvals. Any mismatch must be listed with the relevant clause and sent to the treaty owner for resolution.

Ambiguous wording must not be silently interpreted by an automated system. The reviewer records the competing interpretations, source clauses, financial effect, and required legal or underwriting referral.

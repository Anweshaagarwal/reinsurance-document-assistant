# Synthetic claims triage procedure

This synthetic document is for software demonstration only. On receipt of a claim notification, the claims team records the event date, policy or treaty reference, claimant, loss description, estimated amount, jurisdiction, and supporting documents.

Claims involving possible fraud, sanctions, litigation, medical information, severe bodily injury, or a material coverage dispute require specialist review. The system may identify the referral condition but cannot make the final decision.

The case owner reviews reserves when new evidence changes the expected loss, recovery, or expense. Every reserve change requires a reason, supporting evidence, and an audit record.

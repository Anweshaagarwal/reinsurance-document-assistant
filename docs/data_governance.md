# Synthetic data and AI governance notes

This synthetic document is for software demonstration only. Access to personal, medical, claims, and commercially sensitive information follows least-privilege rules and approved business purposes. Data must not be sent to an external model unless the service, region, retention terms, and contract have been approved.

The AI system must cite its sources, distinguish retrieved evidence from generated text, and state when the evidence is insufficient. It cannot make final underwriting, pricing, claims, or legal decisions by itself.

Teams test retrieval quality, unsupported claims, prompt-injection resistance, access controls, and performance before release. They record model versions, prompts, retrieved sources, user actions, overrides, and material errors for audit and monitoring.

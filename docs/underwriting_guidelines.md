# Synthetic underwriting guidelines

This synthetic document is for software demonstration only. Before reviewing a property risk, the underwriter checks the insured location, occupancy, construction type, insured values, loss history, requested limits, deductibles, and exposure to natural hazards.

The underwriter records exclusions, material assumptions, coverage restrictions, and the evidence used for each conclusion. Each exclusion is linked to its source wording. Missing values or conflicting documents must be flagged rather than inferred.

A referral is required when requested limits exceed delegated authority, the loss history shows an unexplained deterioration, a prohibited occupancy appears, or the available information does not support a defensible view of the risk.
